# PROMETHEUS P1.3K — Genesis Comparator Route Decision & Control-Reservation Gate

Status: **DECISION GATE — NO SOURCE-DESIGNATED UNTREATED INTERNAL POLYGON; INTERNAL RESERVATION REQUIRES EXPLICIT STEWARD DESIGNATION BEFORE FOCAL TREATMENT**  
Date: 2026-09-30

## 1. Decision reached from current evidence

The combined evidence set now includes:

- surveyed ExperimentalSystemBoundary A8D: **3360.875 m²**;
- vector irrigation-project DXF with seven named sector valves and distributed irrigation linework;
- source-defined seven irrigation-sector areas;
- source-defined swale/hydrological influence estimate;
- source-defined building/land-transformation quantities;
- regenerative management planned across the seven sectors.

The source set does **not** identify any closed polygon explicitly designated as an untreated scientific control.

Therefore P1.3K records:

**No internal contemporaneous control polygon is source-established or frozen.**

This is a design-state finding, not a finding that an internal control is impossible.

## 2. Why arithmetic residual cannot be promoted

P1.3I calculated an arithmetic residual of approximately **1693.90–1743.33 m²** outside the reported seven sector areas, depending on the unresolved S5 area.

That residual cannot be promoted to control because current evidence does not establish that it is:

- one contiguous usable land polygon;
- free from irrigation infrastructure or wetting;
- outside swale/hydrological influence;
- free from cover-crop, compost, mulching, planting or other regenerative management;
- comparable in baseline soil/topographic state;
- operationally reservable for the primary comparison window.

The relation's term `superficie agricola inalterata` refers to transformation by building works and does not establish scientific untreated status.

## 3. Internal-control route: explicit reservation requirement

The internal route remains available only through an explicit prospective reservation decision.

Before focal intervention contaminates the candidate, the steward/project must designate a specific land polygon that will remain outside the focal treatment for the preregistered comparison window.

The designation must answer, at minimum:

- polygon/location;
- whether irrigation will be prohibited;
- whether imported fertile soil will be prohibited;
- whether swale/earthwork will be absent;
- whether compost/amendments will be prohibited;
- whether cover crops/mulch/planting will be prohibited or standardized;
- what minimum safety/erosion management remains allowed;
- how long the reservation can remain operationally stable.

Without this prospective decision, the internal-control route cannot reach J-INT.

## 5. External-reference fallback specification

If no internal control is deliberately reserved before focal treatment, P1.3K directs the program to the external-reference route.

An external candidate must be selected before confirmatory outcome interpretation and screened for:

1. same local climatic/bioregional context;
2. comparable soil/parent-material evidence;
3. comparable slope/topographic and hydrologic position;
4. comparable prior disturbance/land-use state;
5. comparable starting vegetation/ground-cover condition;
6. absence of the focal Genesis intervention;
7. stable management over the observation window;
8. repeated-access feasibility;
9. evidence provenance and access/consent;
10. identical primary-endpoint SOPs.

External adjacency does not imply equivalence.

## 6. Baseline protocol consequence

Because comparator geometry remains open, exact confirmatory sampling coordinates remain blocked.

However, baseline preparation can proceed for method qualification and feasibility under an explicitly labelled **pilot/feasibility phase**, provided those observations are not later misrepresented as frozen confirmatory sampling.

The minimum preparation package remains:

- infiltration method qualification;
- aggregate-stability method qualification;
- SOC sampling/lab method selection;
- bulk-density method if SOC stock is intended;
- soil-moisture companion measurement;
- ground-cover documentation;
- disturbance/intervention ledger;
- weather/rainfall and irrigation input record.

## 8. Required human/field decision before P1.3L

One factual decision is now required from the site steward:

> **Will a defined portion of the Genesis site be intentionally reserved from the focal regenerative treatment long enough to serve as a contemporaneous scientific control?**

If **YES**:
- identify it on the CAD/site plan;

- run C1–C5;
- proceed to J-INT.

If **NO**:
- formally fail the internal-control route;
- proceed to J-EXT and matched external-reference selection.

This choice must be prospective. It cannot be reconstructed after treatment from whichever residual area gives a preferred result.
