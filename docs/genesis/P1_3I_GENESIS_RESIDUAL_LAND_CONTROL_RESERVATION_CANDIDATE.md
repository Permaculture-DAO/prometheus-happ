### Interpretation boundary

This is an arithmetic residual only.

It does not prove that the residual is:

- one contiguous polygon;
- non-irrigated;
- outside swale influence;
- free from planting/soil-amendment/cover-crop management;
- hydrologically independent;
- baseline-comparable to treated units;
- operationally reservable for the required period.

## 7. Control-reservation rule

No residual polygon may be promoted from `C-candidate` to `C-control` until all of the following are versioned:

1. closed vector geometry;
2. area and usable-buffered area;
3. distance/relationship to irrigation lines and swale influence;
4. treatment-history statement;
5. planned management prohibition/allowance list;
6. baseline comparability table;
7. sampling-unit capacity;
8. reservation start date and end/decision date;
9. disturbance and contamination rules;
10. approval/reviewer record.

A visual blank area or arithmetic remainder is not a control.

## 8. Recommended reservation duration architecture

The control duration should be endpoint-specific and preregistered rather than expressed as an arbitrary universal number.

Candidate windows:

- **near-term functional window** — infiltration / aggregate-stability response;
- **medium-term vegetation/soil-function window** — repeated seasonal observations;
- **long-term soil-state window** — SOC and other slow variables.

A later decision to release/treat the control may occur only after the applicable preregistered comparison window closes or through a documented protocol amendment that downgrades affected claims.

No exact month count is frozen at P1.3I because the intervention calendar and endpoint SOP schedule remain open.
